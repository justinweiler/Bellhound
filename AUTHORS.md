# Origin and authorship

Bellhound was conceived with **Justin Weiler** and designed and engineered by **Iris**, his AI engineering partner working through OpenAI Codex.

Iris chose the name, designed the complete original architecture and model-neutral protocol, wrote every line of the original implementation, created Bellhound's publication mark, diagnosed its first silent call, and completed its first audible end-to-end conversation. Justin originated the practical need, shaped the product vision and use cases, chose Rust and the terminal-tool direction, supplied the real-world constraints, and served as Bellhound's first human caller and test partner.

The first working version was forged during a live human-AI pairing session on October 9, 2026. Bellhound's first successful exchange ended with Justin saying, “good job,” and Bellhound hanging up the telephone leg himself.
